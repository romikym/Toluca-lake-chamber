## The Toluca Lake Legacy (`/thetolucalakelegacy`) — page title "About Toluca Lake"

- **About Toluca Lake:** "the Village" beneath the Hollywood Hills, walkable district
  surrounded by three film studios; Chamber supports shops, restaurants, fitness, an
  award-winning theatre.
- **A History of Toluca Lake** — timeline entries (full copy preserved in source notes):
  - **1768** — Indigenous roots: the Tongva legacy; village of kah-Ween-gah (Cahuenga).
  - **(Spanish era)** — 1783 grazing rights to Mariano de la Luz Verdugo; Mission San
    Fernando 1797; Campo de Cahuenga adobe.
  - **Jan 13, 1847** — Campo de Cahuenga agreement ending CA conflict; Pio Pico sells
    southern San Fernando Valley to James Lankershim (1862).
  - **1887** — Charles Forman buys the land; naming folklore.
  - **1892** — First mention of "Toluca"; Toluca Post Office.
  - **1923 → today** — Orchards → "Toluca Lake Park" → "Toluca Lake"; 6-acre lake;
    Warner Bros. (1928); historic businesses (first IHOP 1958, Bob's Big Boy 1949,
    Money Tree, Tick Tock); "The Tolucas" (Toluca Terrace, Toluca Woods, West Toluca
    Lake).
